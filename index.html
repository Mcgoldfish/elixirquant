EDUCATIONAL BASELINE

Moving-average crossover

Hold the asset when its fast moving average exceeds its slow average. Otherwise, hold cash.

Long only · fractional shares · no leverage · zero interest on cash. Yesterday’s signal executes at today’s close with slippage. Final holdings are marked to market, not liquidated.

Methodology & limitations

Simple moving averages use closing prices. Signals are delayed one session. Transaction costs apply on each entry and exit. Drawdown uses daily marked equity. Buy & hold is a frictionless reference. The simulator omits taxes, market impact, liquidity constraints, dividends and corporate actions. Synthetic outcomes do not establish real-world profitability.
